Why I Made This Website

For Starters, I always wanted my own website to have fun with, so that’s why I coded JosephShipley.Com, its more of a fun hobbyist website more than anything, I love learning about obscure animation history, music history, physical media history and much more, so I wanna put together what I learnt in one place, and inside a binder of course (whenever I get the chance) this also gives me a reason to not be on social media as much as I used to be, I am not the biggest fan of it and I tend to stay away from it as much as I possibly can, don’t wanna waste my life on a app that won’t last forever, right?, but yeah JosephShipley.Com serves as my
Home for just me messing around and doing whatever, it’s my personal corner online where I can do whatever I want freely

Why Leave Social Media?:

It’s gotten way worse recently, some will agree with me, but honestly I just don’t wanna waste my life on a app that won’t last forever anyways, you know what I mean right?, I wanna have fun and learn about the most obscure animation, Music, And Physical Media History as much as I possibly can, in life you are supposed to have fun, not scroll on screens for the rest of eternity, that doesn’t give you satisfaction, but learning about stuff you wanna learn about does, these three things have always been a big role in my life and now I’m on a journey to learn about a bunch of stuff I never knew about, life should be about exploring stuff you’ve never saw or heard of before, that’s the thing about life that I like about

Distractions:

It’s always been a distraction to me, these companies have always found a way to keep you on the app, not me though, it just doesn’t make sense to me to keep these apps in all honesty, I wanna accomplish as much as I can when learning about obscure things and such

But yeah that’s why I made JosephShipley.Com, it’s a fun hobbyist website made for fun, I’m updating It To V1.2 right now, as you can see the physical media sections and etc have nice grids to them and I tend to keep it that way, I’m hyped for what 2027 has in store for this website honestly
